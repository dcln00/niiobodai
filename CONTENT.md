# Study Abroad

## Prepare Before You Arrive

Studying abroad is one of the biggest investments you'll ever make.

Yet many students spend months preparing their university applications while spending almost no time preparing for life after landing.

That's where I come in.

I help international students understand the realities of living in America before they arrive, covering everything from finances and housing to employment, transportation, healthcare, networking, and career development.

When you're prepared, you spend less time struggling and more time succeeding.
